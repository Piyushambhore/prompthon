💰 Benefit Entitlement
₹6,000 per year direct income support in 3 equal installments of ₹2,000.

  • All landholding farmer families having cultivable land in their names.

  • Aadhaar Card (Linked with Mobile)
  • Land Record Certificate (Khata / Khasra / 7/12)

  • Step 1: Visit official portal (pmkisan.gov.in) or visit nearest CSC Centre.
  • Step 2: Enter Aadhaar number and complete OTP biometric authentication.
  • Step 3: Upload land records and submit for District Officer verification.
